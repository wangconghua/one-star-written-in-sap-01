One original one-line 1-star review, written in the voice of a houseplant
about the human who owns it. The plant's grievance is the centre: its owner
calls it "low-maintenance", forgets it for three weeks, then drowns it like
he's putting out a fire — and acts betrayed when it drops a leaf. The turn
lands in the last clause: the plant isn't low-maintenance, it's a hostage
with good branding. No stock phrases about drama queens, dramatic wilting
or being ignored; the detail is specific to a plant's life — watering,
dropping a leaf, being left on a schedule its owner keeps forgetting.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(207 characters, one line — inside the gate's 1–279 character limit).
